Sample clinical questions

Normal Pregnancy & Labor · Moderate

A 26-year-old primigravida at 32 weeks of gestation presents for a routine antenatal visit. She complains of mild shortness of breath on exertion and occasional palpitations. Her blood pressure is 110/70 mmHg, and heart rate is 88 bpm. Cardiac auscultation reveals a grade 2/6 systolic ejection murmur at the left sternal border. ECG shows a slight left axis deviation. Which of the following is the most likely physiological explanation for these findings?

Complications of Pregnancy · Hard
