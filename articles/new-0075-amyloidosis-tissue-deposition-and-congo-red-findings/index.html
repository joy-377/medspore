01

Pathophysiology & identification

  • Amyloid represents extracellular deposition of misfolded fibrillar proteins that may damage organs.
  • Congo red staining shows characteristic apple-green birefringence under polarized light when performed and interpreted correctly.
02

Diagnostic and treatment connections

  • AL amyloidosis involves immunoglobulin light chains; AA is associated with chronic inflammatory processes.
  • Cardiac, renal, neural and gastrointestinal involvement can occur depending on amyloid type.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Typing the amyloid is vital: a positive stain does not identify the specific amyloid protein by itself.
