Recognize the core pattern

  • Anterior epistaxis commonly arises from the anterior septal vascular plexus.
  • Posterior epistaxis is more likely in older adults and may require specialized management or airway precautions.
02

Clinical examination and interpretation

  • Initial measures include sitting forward, direct pressure to the soft nose and assessing hemodynamic stability.
  • Anticoagulants, trauma and recurrent bleeding change management decisions.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not tilt the head backward; this can allow blood to flow into the pharynx.
