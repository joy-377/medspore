Vaccine Types, Cold Chain & Contraindications

Live vs Killed vaccines, ILR vs Deep Freezer temperatures, and VVM...

High-Yield Tables · Immunization

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

Classification:
• Live Attenuated: BCG, OPV, Measles, Rubella, Mumps, Yellow Fever, Rotavirus, Varicella, JE (Live SA-14-14-2).
• Killed / Inactivated: IPV (Salk), Rabies, Pertussis (whole cell), Hepatitis A, Cholera.
• Toxoid: Tetanus, Diphtheria.
• Subunit / Recombinant: Hepatitis B (HBsAg), HPV, acellular Pertussis (aP).

Cold Chain Equipment & Storage:
• Standard Temperature: +2°C to +8°C for all vaccines at PHC/district level.
• Deep Freezer (-15°C to -25°C): Used exclusively for preparing ice packs and storing OPV/Measles at national/regional stores.
• Ice-Lined Refrigerator (ILR): Most vital equipment at PHC level. Bottom holds heat-sensitive vaccines (OPV, Measles); top holds freeze-sensitive vaccines (HepB, DPT, TT, Pentavalent).
• Shake Test: Done to identify if freeze-sensitive vaccines (DPT, Td, HepB) were damaged by sub-zero freezing.

Vaccine Vial Monitor (VVM):
• Usable: Inner square is lighter than outer circle.
• Discard: Inner square matches or is darker than outer circle, or past expiry date.
