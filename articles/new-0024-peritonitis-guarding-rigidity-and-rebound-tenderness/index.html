01

Recognize the pattern

  • Peritonitis may cause involuntary guarding, rigidity, rebound tenderness and pain on movement.
  • A rigid abdomen in an unstable patient raises concern for serious intra-abdominal pathology such as perforation.
02

What this finding does—and does not—mean

  • Analgesia and resuscitation should not be withheld while obtaining clinical and surgical assessment.
  • Peritoneal signs may be subtle in older adults, immunosuppressed patients or those receiving steroids.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Rebound tenderness alone cannot establish the exact cause or site of abdominal pathology.
