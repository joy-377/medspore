Quick Revision

  • Susceptible does not mean every drug is suitable at every infection site or in every patient.
  • Culture context, specimen quality and local guidelines prevent inappropriate treatment of colonization.
01

Pathophysiology & identification

  • MIC measures the lowest tested antimicrobial concentration preventing visible growth under standardized conditions.
  • Clinical breakpoints classify susceptibility using microbiology, pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics and clinical evidence.
02

Diagnostic and treatment connections

  • Susceptible does not mean every drug is suitable at every infection site or in every patient.
  • Culture context, specimen quality and local guidelines prevent inappropriate treatment of colonization.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • An MIC number cannot be interpreted without organism-drug-specific breakpoints and methods.
