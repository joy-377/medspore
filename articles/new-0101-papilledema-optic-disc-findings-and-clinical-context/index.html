01

Recognize the core pattern

  • Papilledema is optic disc swelling associated with raised intracranial pressure, but disc swelling has other causes.
  • Blurred disc margins, vessel obscuration and hemorrhages can be clues on fundus examination.
02

Clinical examination and interpretation

  • Assess headache, neurologic signs, vision and intracranial causes before procedures such as lumbar puncture.
  • Optic disc appearance is not interchangeable with visual function; acuity can be preserved early.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Not every swollen optic disc is papilledema, and a normal fundus does not exclude early raised intracranial pressure.
