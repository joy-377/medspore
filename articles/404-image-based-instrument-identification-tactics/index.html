Image-Based & Instrument Identification Tactics

Radiology slices, histopathology slides, and surgical instruments...

Exam Strategy · INI-CET Strategy

Step 1: Read the Vignette Before Staring at the Image
Images in INI-CET are often confirmatory rather than purely diagnostic. The patient's age, gender, and clinical presentation narrow the differential to 1–2 possibilities before analyzing the CT or biopsy slide.

Step 2: Know the Classical Buzzwords:
• Onion-skinning: Ewing Sarcoma (bone) or Hyperplastic arteriolosclerosis (renal).
• Sunburst pattern / Codman triangle: Osteosarcoma.
• Soap-bubble appearance: Giant Cell Tumor (epiphyseal).
• Signet ring cells: Gastric Adenocarcinoma (Diffuse type).

Instruments & Sutures: Review standard surgical trays (Allis, Babcocks, Spencer Wells) and suture materials (absorbable vs non-absorbable, natural vs synthetic).
