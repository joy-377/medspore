Top 5 Dangerous Drug-Drug Interactions

Combinations that can trigger life-threatening crises in the ward...

Clinical Tips · Prescription Pearls

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

Never combine these drugs without careful consideration and monitoring:

  • 1. Sildenafil (Viagra) + Nitrates: Both increase cGMP, causing massive, irreversible, and potentially fatal hypotension. Always check for PDE-5 inhibitor use before giving sublingual NTG for chest pain.
  • 2. Warfarin + Amiodarone / Fluconazole / Bactrim: These are potent CYP450 inhibitors. They block Warfarin metabolism, causing a sudden spike in INR and massive bleeding risk.
  • 3. ACE Inhibitors + Spironolactone: Both drugs retain potassium. Combining them significantly increases the risk of severe hyperkalemia and arrhythmias.
  • 4. SSRIs (Fluoxetine) + MAOIs / Tramadol: High risk of precipitating Serotonin Syndrome (hyperthermia, clonus, tremors).
  • 5. Clopidogrel + Omeprazole: Omeprazole inhibits CYP2C19, the exact enzyme needed to convert Clopidogrel (a prodrug) into its active form. This renders the Clopidogrel useless, leading to stent thrombosis. (Use Pantoprazole instead).
