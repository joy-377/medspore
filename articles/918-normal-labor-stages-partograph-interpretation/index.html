Normal Labor Stages & Partograph Interpretation

Active phase, alert/action lines, cervical dilatation, and fetal descent...

Final Year (Part 2) · OBG

Stages of Labor:
• First Stage: From onset of true labor pains to full cervical dilatation (10 cm). Divided into Latent phase (up to 4 cm, slow) and Active phase (4 cm to 10 cm, rapid progression at $ge 1$ cm/hr in nulliparas).

The World Health Organization (WHO) Partograph:
• Designed to monitor labor progress and prevent obstructed labor.
• Alert Line: Drawn from 4 cm dilatation at a rate of 1 cm/hour. If the cervical dilatation curve crosses to the right of the alert line, it warns of slow progress.
• Action Line: Parallel to and 4 hours to the right of the alert line. If the plot crosses the action line, immediate clinical intervention (augmentation with oxytocin, or Cesarean section for cephalopelvic disproportion) is mandatory.

• Second Stage: From full cervical dilatation to delivery of the baby (Duration: ~2 hours in nulliparas, ~1 hour in multiparas).

• Third Stage: From delivery of the baby to expulsion of the placenta and membranes (Managed actively with oxytocin to prevent PPH).
