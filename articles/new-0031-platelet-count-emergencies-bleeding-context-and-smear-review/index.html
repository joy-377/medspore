01

Interpret the result in context

  • A low platelet count may reflect reduced production, increased destruction or sequestration; bleeding risk varies by cause and context.
  • Confirm unexpected results with a smear to exclude platelet clumping and pseudothrombocytopenia.
02

Clinical interpretation & next steps

  • Look for bleeding, fever, neurologic symptoms, hemolysis and thrombosis before deciding on urgency.
  • Transfusion decisions are situation-specific; suspected thrombotic microangiopathy needs urgent etiologic evaluation.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not treat an isolated automated count as definitive without considering the smear and clinical situation.
