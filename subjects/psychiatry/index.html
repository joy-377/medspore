Sample clinical questions

Schizophrenia & Psychotic Disorders · Moderate

A 24-year-old man is brought to the clinic by his family because he has been locking himself in his room for the past 3 months. He claims that the government is broadcasting his thoughts on the radio and frequently talks to people who are not present. Prior to this, he had no psychiatric history and was functioning well in college. Physical examination and urine toxicology are unremarkable. What is the most likely diagnosis?

Mood Disorders · Easy

A 45-year-old man complains of feeling profoundly sad, exhausted, and unable to concentrate at work. He has lost 5 kg (11 lbs) without trying, wakes up at 3 AM every morning unable to fall back asleep, and feels that his family would be better off without him. For a diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder to be established according to DSM-5 criteria, these symptoms must have been present for at least what minimum duration?

Anxiety & Somatoform Disorders · Easy

A 26-year-old woman comes to the emergency room terrified that she is having a heart attack. She experiences sudden episodes of intense chest pain, palpitations, diaphoresis, and shortness of breath peaking within 10 minutes. Her ECG and troponins are normal. She has had four similar episodes in the past month and now refuses to leave her house for fear of having another attack. What is the most appropriate long-term pharmacological treatment?

Substance Abuse · Moderate

He is picking at the air and claims insects are crawling on his walls. His heart rate is 130 bpm, blood pressure is 170/100 mmHg, and temperature is 38.5 C.
