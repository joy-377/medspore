Pacing, strike rate, and negative marking management in a 3.5-hour exam...

Exam Strategy · NEET PG Strategy

2026 update: NEET PG 2026 used 180 questions, 210 minutes and five time-bound sections of 36 questions with 42 minutes each. Earlier 200-question advice is outdated for this session.

Build an effective practice routine

  • Practise one timed section without leaving it before the clock ends.
  • Prioritise well-supported choices and mark uncertain questions for review within the active section.
  • Once the section closes, do not plan to revisit it. Review errors only after the mock ends.
  • Use short, focused revision sets alongside full-length mocks.

Always cross-check the next exam session’s official information bulletin before finalizing your strategy.
