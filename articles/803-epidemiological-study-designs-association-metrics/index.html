Epidemiological Study Designs & Association Metrics

Case-Control vs Cohort, Odds Ratio, Relative Risk, and Attributable Risk calculations...

Third Year (Part 1) · PSM

Case-Control Study (Retrospective):
• Starting point: Disease status (Cases with disease vs Controls without disease). Works backward to assess prior exposure.
• Best for: Rare diseases and long latency conditions.
• Metric of Association: Odds Ratio (OR) = (a × d) / (b × c) (cross-product ratio). Incidence cannot be calculated directly.
• Major Bias: Recall Bias and Selection Bias.

Cohort Study (Prospective / Follow-up):
• Starting point: Exposure status (Exposed group vs Non-exposed group). Followed forward over time to detect incidence of disease.
• Best for: Rare exposures and evaluating multiple disease outcomes from a single exposure.
• Metric of Association: Relative Risk (RR) = Incidence in Exposed / Incidence in Non-exposed [ (a/(a+b)) / (c/(c+d)) ].
• Public Health Impact Metrics:
  - Attributable Risk (AR%): [ (RR - 1) / RR ] × 100 (proportion of disease in exposed attributable to exposure).
  - Population Attributable Risk (PAR%): Potential reduction in total disease incidence if the risk factor were completely eliminated from the population.
• Major Bias: Loss to follow-up (Attrition bias).
