01

Urea Cycle Disorders

Urea Cycle Disorders:
• Ornithine Transcarbamylase (OTC) Deficiency: Most common urea cycle defect. Only X-linked recessive form (all others autosomal recessive). Features: Severe hyperammonemia, lethargy, respiratory alkalosis. Elevated urine orotic acid with low serum citrulline.
• Carbamoyl Phosphate Synthetase 1 (CPS-1) Deficiency: Identical clinical presentation to OTC, but Urine orotic acid is LOW or absent.

02

Amino Acid Catabolism Disorders

Amino Acid Catabolism Disorders:
• Phenylketonuria (PKU): Deficiency of Phenylalanine Hydroxylase (or BH4 cofactor). Phenylalanine accumulates & converted to phenylketones. Features: Intellectual disability, microcephaly, fair skin/blond hair, mousy / musty body odor.
• Alkaptonuria: Deficiency of Homogentisate Oxidase in tyrosine catabolism. Homogentisic acid accumulates. Features: Urine turns black on standing / alkalization, dark pigmentation of sclera and ear cartilage (Ochronosis), early-onset severe arthritis.
• Maple Syrup Urine Disease (MSUD): Defect in Branched-Chain Alpha-Ketoacid Dehydrogenase (BCKDH) complex. Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine accumulate. Features: Vomiting, CNS depression, sweet burnt-sugar / maple syrup odor of urine.
