Quick Revision

  • Type V (McArdle) is muscle glycogen phosphorylase deficiency associated with exercise intolerance.
  • Differentiate by tissue affected, fasting response, muscle symptoms and enzyme defect rather than the shared word glycogen.
01

Core biomedical mechanism

  • Type I glycogen storage disease (von Gierke) involves glucose-6-phosphatase system dysfunction and fasting hypoglycemia.
  • Type II (Pompe) is lysosomal acid alpha-glucosidase deficiency associated with cardiomyopathy or myopathy.
02

Clinical application

  • Type V (McArdle) is muscle glycogen phosphorylase deficiency associated with exercise intolerance.
  • Differentiate by tissue affected, fasting response, muscle symptoms and enzyme defect rather than the shared word glycogen.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Pompe disease is a lysosomal disorder rather than simply a hepatic glycogen mobilization defect.
