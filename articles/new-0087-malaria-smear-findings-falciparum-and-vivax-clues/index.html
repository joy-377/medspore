Pathophysiology & identification

  • Thick blood films improve sensitivity for malaria parasite detection; thin films help species identification and parasitemia assessment.
  • P. falciparum may show multiple delicate rings in one red cell and appliqué forms; P. vivax often enlarges infected red cells.
02

Diagnostic and treatment connections

  • Repeat sampling may be needed when initial microscopy is negative but clinical suspicion remains high.
  • Severity assessment and local drug-resistance guidance determine management.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Morphology has overlap; do not rely on one smear feature without laboratory quality control.
