Quick Revision

  • Screening identifies children who may need evaluation; a positive screen is not a confirmed autism diagnosis.
  • Hearing assessment is important when speech or response-to-name concerns arise.
01

Essential distinctions

  • Social communication differences, restricted behaviors and developmental concerns merit a comprehensive assessment.
  • Not responding to name, reduced joint attention or loss of language may be warning signs but are not diagnostic by themselves.
