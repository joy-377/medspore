Quick Revision

  • Likelihood ratios are not themselves the posttest probability; odds-based updating is required.
  • Spectrum effects and test thresholds can change real-world test performance.
01

Recognize the core pattern

  • Sensitivity and specificity describe test characteristics; likelihood ratios link test results to pretest probability.
  • A positive likelihood ratio greater than one tends to increase probability, while a negative ratio below one tends to decrease it.
02

Clinical examination and interpretation

  • Likelihood ratios are not themselves the posttest probability; odds-based updating is required.
  • Spectrum effects and test thresholds can change real-world test performance.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not interpret an isolated test result without an estimate of prior clinical probability.
