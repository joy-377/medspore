Recognize the question type

  • Identify the question stem's actual task: diagnosis, mechanism, best next test, immediate stabilization or definitive treatment.
  • Separate decisive clues from background details; age, time course and one discriminating finding often guide the answer.
02

Use this in timed practice

  • Before reading options, articulate a one-sentence hypothesis, then test it against competing choices.
  • Use the final line of the stem to prevent answering a different question from the one asked.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not select a disease name when the prompt asks for immediate management.
