Complete Blood Count (CBC) & Anemia Indices

MCV, RDW, Mentzer Index, and reticulocyte production index rules...

Critical Lab Values · Hematology & ABG

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

Microcytic Anemia (MCV < 80 fL):
• Iron Deficiency Anemia (IDA): Low serum Iron, Low Ferritin, High TIBC, High RDW.
• Thalassemia Trait: Normal/High Ferritin, Normal TIBC, Normal RDW.
• Mentzer Index (MCV / RBC count): >13 suggests IDA; <13 strongly points toward Beta-Thalassemia Trait.

Macrocytic Anemia (MCV > 100 fL):
• Megaloblastic: Vit B12 or Folate deficiency (hypersegmented neutrophils >5 lobes).
• Non-megaloblastic: Alcoholism, Hypothyroidism, Liver disease, Reticulocytosis.

Reticulocyte Production Index (RPI):
Corrected Retic Count = Retic % × (Patient Hct / 45). RPI = Corrected Retic / Maturation Correction Factor. RPI > 2 indicates hyperproliferative (hemolysis/hemorrhage); RPI < 2 indicates hypoproliferative (bone marrow failure/deficiency).
