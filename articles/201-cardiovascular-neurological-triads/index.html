Triads & Signs · Medical Triads

  • Beck's Triad (Cardiac Tamponade): 1. Hypotension, 2. Muffled heart sounds, 3. Distended Jugular Vein (JVD). Pathophysiology: Fluid in pericardial sac restricts heart filling, dropping stroke volume.
  • Cushing's Triad (Raised Intracranial Pressure): 1. Hypertension (with widened pulse pressure), 2. Bradycardia, 3. Irregular respiration (Cheyne-Stokes). Clue: It's a late, ominous sign of brain herniation.
  • Virchow's Triad (DVT / Pulmonary Embolism): 1. Venous stasis (bedridden), 2. Endothelial injury (surgery/trauma), 3. Hypercoagulability (Factor V Leiden, OCPs, Cancer).
  • Wernicke's Triad (Thiamine Deficiency): 1. Confusion (Encephalopathy), 2. Oculomotor dysfunction (Nystagmus/CN VI palsy), 3. Ataxia. Treatment: ALWAYS give IV Thiamine BEFORE Glucose.
