Quick Revision

  • Disability includes conscious level, pupils and bedside glucose; exposure looks for bleeding, rash, trauma and temperature-related threats.
  • Use a team approach with closed-loop communication, early escalation and definitive help when instability persists.
01

Recognize the situation

  • ABCDE stands for Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Disability and Exposure; reassess after every intervention.
  • An obstructed airway, severe hypoxemia or massive bleeding threatens life before detailed diagnostic labeling.
02

Clinical decisions & differential

  • Disability includes conscious level, pupils and bedside glucose; exposure looks for bleeding, rash, trauma and temperature-related threats.
  • Use a team approach with closed-loop communication, early escalation and definitive help when instability persists.
03

Memory aid

  • ABCDE: Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Disability, Exposure — then reassess.
  • Prioritize life-threatening abnormalities while simultaneously calling for appropriate assistance.
04

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Treat the life threat identified at each step rather than completing the alphabet before intervening.
