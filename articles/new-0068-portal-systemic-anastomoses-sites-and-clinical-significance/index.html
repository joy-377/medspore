Quick Revision

  • Esophageal varices are a potentially severe source of upper gastrointestinal bleeding.
  • Management depends on the cause of portal hypertension and preventive or acute bleeding pathways.
01

Core biomedical mechanism

  • Portal-systemic anastomoses connect portal venous tributaries to systemic veins at sites such as esophagus, rectum and abdominal wall.
  • Portal hypertension may lead to gastroesophageal varices and splenomegaly.
02

Clinical application

  • Esophageal varices are a potentially severe source of upper gastrointestinal bleeding.
  • Management depends on the cause of portal hypertension and preventive or acute bleeding pathways.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Hemorrhoids are not synonymous with rectal varices, even though both involve the anorectal region.
