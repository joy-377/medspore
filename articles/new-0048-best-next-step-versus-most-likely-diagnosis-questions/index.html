  • Immediate stabilization and definitive treatment are different phases of care.
  • Look for words such as first, next, initial and most appropriate to set the response priority.
01

Recognize the question type

  • Most likely diagnosis asks for the best explanation of the evidence; best next step asks for the immediate decision under the circumstances.
  • A stable patient may need testing, while an unstable patient may need resuscitation before definitive diagnostics.
02

Use this in timed practice

  • Immediate stabilization and definitive treatment are different phases of care.

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not choose the definitive procedure when an airway or circulatory emergency takes priority.
