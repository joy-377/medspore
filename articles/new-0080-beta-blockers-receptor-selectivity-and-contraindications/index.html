Quick Revision

  • Nonselective beta blockade can precipitate bronchospasm in susceptible patients.
  • Indications and risks vary in heart failure, ischemic heart disease, arrhythmia and pregnancy.
01

Pathophysiology & identification

  • Beta-1 receptors influence heart rate and contractility; beta-2 receptors influence bronchial and vascular smooth muscle.
  • Cardioselective beta blockers preferentially block beta-1 at usual doses but selectivity is incomplete and dose-dependent.
02

Diagnostic and treatment connections

  • Nonselective beta blockade can precipitate bronchospasm in susceptible patients.
  • Indications and risks vary in heart failure, ischemic heart disease, arrhythmia and pregnancy.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not abruptly stop long-term beta blockers without a plan because rebound sympathetic effects can occur.
