01

Core biomedical mechanism

  • The inguinal canal conveys the spermatic cord in males and round ligament in females, with the ilioinguinal nerve traversing part of it.
  • An indirect inguinal hernia enters the deep ring lateral to the inferior epigastric vessels; a direct hernia projects medially through Hesselbach triangle.
02

Clinical application

  • Identify the inguinal ligament, deep and superficial rings and posterior wall when interpreting groin anatomy.
  • Femoral hernias pass below the inguinal ligament and carry important strangulation risk.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • A lump's relation to the inferior epigastric vessels is an anatomical distinction, not safely established by surface examination alone.
