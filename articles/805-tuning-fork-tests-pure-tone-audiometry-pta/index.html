Tuning Fork Tests & Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA)

Rinne and Weber tuning fork tests help differentiate conductive from sensorineural hearing patterns when interpreted with history and audiometry.

Quick Revision

  • In unilateral conductive hearing loss, Weber sound generally lateralizes toward the affected ear.
  • In unilateral sensorineural loss, Weber sound generally lateralizes toward the better-hearing ear.
  • Rinne compares air and bone conduction on the tested side; formal audiometry confirms the degree and type of hearing loss.
01

Weber: lateralization

  • Conductive asymmetry often sends Weber to the poorer-hearing ear.
  • Sensorineural asymmetry often sends Weber to the better-hearing ear.
02

Rinne: air versus bone

  • Air conduction normally exceeds bone conduction; a reversal can suggest a conductive component.
  • Interpret reliability according to test technique and hearing severity.
03

Exam trap

  • Tuning-fork tests are screening clues and can be misleading in severe or mixed losses.
  • Do not equate a single bedside result with a complete audiogram.
