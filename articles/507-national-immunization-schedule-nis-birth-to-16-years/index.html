National Immunization Schedule (NIS): Birth to 16 Years

High-Yield Tables · Immunization

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

At Birth (0-15 Days):
• BCG: 0.05 mL (0.1 mL if >1 month), Intradermal (Left upper arm).
• OPV (Zero dose): 2 drops, Oral.
• Hepatitis B (Birth dose): 0.5 mL, IM (Anterolateral aspect of mid-thigh within 24h).

At 6, 10, and 14 Weeks:
• Pentavalent (DPT + HepB + Hib): 0.5 mL, IM (Mid-thigh).
• Rotavirus (RVV): 5 drops (liquid) or 2.5 mL (lyophilized), Oral.
• fIPV (Fractional IPV): 0.1 mL, Intradermal (Right upper arm at 6 and 14 weeks).
• Pneumococcal Conjugate (PCV): 0.5 mL, IM (Mid-thigh at 6 and 14 weeks, booster at 9 months).

At 9-12 Months:
• MR (Measles-Rubella 1st dose): 0.5 mL, Subcutaneous (Right upper arm).
• Japanese Encephalitis (JE 1st dose in endemic districts): 0.5 mL, Subcutaneous.
• Vitamin A (1st dose): 1 mL (1 Lakh IU), Oral.

Boosters (16-24 Months & Beyond):
• DPT Booster-1, MR-2, OPV Booster: At 16-24 months.
• Vitamin A (Doses 2 to 9): 2 mL (2 Lakh IU) every 6 months up to 5 years (Total 9 doses / 17 Lakh IU).
• DPT Booster-2: At 5-6 years.
• Td (Tetanus-adult Diphtheria): At 10 years and 16 years (replaces plain TT).
