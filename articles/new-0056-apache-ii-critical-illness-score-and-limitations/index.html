Quick Revision

  • Scores can vary according to the time window and data used.
  • Performance depends on case mix and calibration, so published outcome estimates are not universally transferable.
01

Essential distinctions

  • APACHE II uses acute physiology, age and chronic health factors to characterize severity in critically ill cohorts.
  • It is a prognostic and risk-adjustment tool, not a diagnosis or bedside command to begin a particular treatment.

What is APACHE II primarily used for?

For medical learning and exam revision. Clinical decisions require individualized assessment and current local protocols.
