Infectious Diseases & Neurological Drug of Choice

Definitive treatments for MRSA, VRE, Status Epilepticus, and Trigeminal Neuralgia...

High-Yield Tables · Drug of Choice

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

Infectious Diseases:
• MRSA (Methicillin-Resistant S. aureus): Vancomycin (IV) or Daptomycin / Linezolid.
• VRE (Vancomycin-Resistant Enterococcus): Linezolid or Daptomycin.
• Pseudomonas aeruginosa: Piperacillin-Tazobactam (Pip-Taz) or Ceftazidime / Cefepime.
• Syphilis (all stages): Benzathine Penicillin G.
• Cerebral Malaria (P. falciparum): IV Artesunate.
• Clostridioides difficile (Colitis): Oral Vancomycin or Oral Fidaxomicin.

Neurology & Psychiatry:
• Status Epilepticus (Initial): IV Lorazepam (0.1 mg/kg) followed by IV Fosphenytoin/Levetiracetam.
• Trigeminal Neuralgia: Carbamazepine.
• Absence Seizures: Ethosuximide (Valproate if combined with tonic-clonic).
• Delirium Tremens (Alcohol Withdrawal): Chlordiazepoxide or Diazepam.
• Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome: Dantrolene or Bromocriptine.
• Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD): SSRIs (Fluoxetine, Sertraline) or Clomipramine.
