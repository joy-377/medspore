01

Recognize the pattern

  • Cerebral edema may produce sulcal effacement, reduced gray-white differentiation and compressed ventricles on CT.
  • Midline shift signals mass effect but is not a direct measurement of intracranial pressure.
02

What this finding does—and does not—mean

  • Assess for herniation signs, reduced consciousness and underlying stroke, hemorrhage, mass or infection.
  • A normal early CT may not reflect the full evolution of ischemia or swelling.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not equate the amount of radiologic shift with clinical safety; examination and trend matter.
