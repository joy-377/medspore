01

Clinical syndrome and red flags

  • Gestational diabetes is hyperglycemia first recognized during pregnancy and is associated with maternal and fetal complications.
  • Screening timing and diagnostic thresholds vary by accepted national or institutional criteria.
02

Differentiate and manage

  • Nutrition, activity, glucose monitoring and pharmacotherapy when indicated support management.
  • Postpartum glucose follow-up matters because later type 2 diabetes risk is increased.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not treat a single screening strategy as universal across all countries and societies.

Why is postpartum follow-up important after gestational diabetes?
