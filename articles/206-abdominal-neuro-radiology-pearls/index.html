Abdominal & Neuro Radiology Pearls

Classic abdominal and brain imaging signs connect shape to disease but still need full-context interpretation.

Quick Revision

  • Coffee-bean appearance can be associated with sigmoid volvulus; a bird-beak narrowing may occur in achalasia.
  • An apple-core colonic lesion suggests a constricting process requiring further investigation.
  • Extra-axial hemorrhage patterns on CT are classically lens-shaped for epidural and crescentic for subdural bleeding, with exceptions.
01

Obstruction signs in abdominal imaging

  • Coffee-bean sign: a markedly dilated twisted sigmoid colon in some presentations of sigmoid volvulus.
  • Bird-beak sign: tapered gastroesophageal junction on contrast studies of achalasia; other disorders can mimic it.
02

Luminal narrowing patterns

  • Apple-core narrowing is a classic colonic contrast-imaging clue for annular colorectal carcinoma but is not by itself a histologic diagnosis.
  • A string-like narrowed segment in ileal imaging can be seen in Crohn disease, interpreted alongside other findings.
03

Head CT extra-axial blood

  • Epidural hematoma is often biconvex; subdural blood is often crescentic.
  • Suture boundaries, anatomical location and chronicity affect the imaging appearance and differential.
04

Exam trap

  • A classic image silhouette is a clue, not a universal rule or replacement for clinical evaluation.
