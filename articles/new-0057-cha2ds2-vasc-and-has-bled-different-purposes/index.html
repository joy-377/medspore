Quick Revision

  • Bleeding-risk assessment should prompt risk modification rather than automatically withholding indicated anticoagulation.
  • Guideline thresholds and use of sex-based points vary with updated pathways; verify the current regional recommendations.
01

Essential distinctions

  • CHA2DS2-VASc estimates thromboembolic risk in atrial fibrillation; HAS-BLED identifies modifiable bleeding risk factors.
  • The two scores answer different questions and should not be directly subtracted from each other.
