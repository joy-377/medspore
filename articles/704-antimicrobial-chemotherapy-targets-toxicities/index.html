01

Mechanism of Action Targets

Mechanism of Action Targets:
• Cell Wall Synthesis Inhibitors: Beta-lactams (Penicillins, Cephalosporins, Carbapenems) bind PBPs and block peptidoglycan cross-linking; Vancomycin binds D-Ala-D-Ala terminus of cell wall precursors.
• 30S Ribosomal Inhibitors: Aminoglycosides (Gentamicin, Amikacin - bactericidal, cause misreading of mRNA); Tetracyclines (Doxycycline - bacteriostatic, block tRNA attachment to A site).
• 50S Ribosomal Inhibitors: Macrolides (Azithromycin), Clindamycin, Chloramphenicol, Linezolid (blocks initiation complex formation).
• DNA Synthesis / Replication: Fluoroquinolones (Ciprofloxacin - inhibits DNA Gyrase / Topoisomerase II and IV); Metronidazole (forms toxic free radicals in anaerobes).

02

High-Yield Drug Toxicities

High-Yield Drug Toxicities:
• Vancomycin: Red Man Syndrome (direct histamine release; prevent by slowing infusion rate), Nephrotoxicity, Ototoxicity.
• Aminoglycosides: Ototoxicity (vestibular and cochlear damage), Nephrotoxicity (ATN), Neuromuscular blockade (contraindicated in Myasthenia Gravis).
• Chloramphenicol: Aplastic Anemia, Gray Baby Syndrome (due to deficient UDP-glucuronyltransferase in neonates).
