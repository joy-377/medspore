About MedSpore

MedSpore is an independent medical learning workspace designed for MBBS students and postgraduate exam aspirants. It connects clinically oriented MCQs with explanations, subject topics, progress tracking, revision and educational reading.

Our approach

We focus on active recall, understanding why distractors are incorrect, and consistent practice. This release includes sample questions across 19 subjects, free notes and a curated library of legacy educational articles.

Not an official examination authority

MedSpore is not affiliated with NBEMS, AIIMS, the National Medical Commission, or any examination agency. Test simulators approximate published exam patterns; the sample bank is not a collection of verified previous-year papers.

Clinical content review

Some practice questions and articles were adapted from the earlier MedQ Daily educational site. They are undergoing editorial review and should not be used as a substitute for current clinical guidelines, professional supervision or patient care advice.
