Acute Appendicitis: Alvarado Score & Imaging

Appendicitis is evaluated using the clinical pattern, serial examination, laboratory context and appropriate imaging.

Quick Revision

  • Migrating periumbilical-to-right-lower-quadrant pain is classic, but presentations are variable.
  • Clinical scores such as Alvarado can assist risk stratification; they are not independent proof of appendicitis.
  • Imaging selection depends on age, pregnancy, probability and local expertise.
01

Clinical features

  • Pain often begins centrally and localizes to the right lower abdomen; nausea, anorexia and focal tenderness may occur.
  • Elderly, pregnant and immunosuppressed patients can have atypical findings.
02

Investigation pathway

  • Combine examination, blood tests and appropriately selected ultrasound or cross-sectional imaging.
  • Consider gynecologic, urinary and gastrointestinal mimics when evaluating right lower-quadrant pain.
03

Exam trap

  • Do not diagnose or exclude appendicitis from a single fever cutoff or Alvarado point total.
  • Worsening pain, systemic instability or peritoneal signs require urgent reassessment.
