01

Clinical syndrome and red flags

  • Biliary colic typically produces episodic pain from transient cystic duct obstruction without persistent inflammation.
  • Acute cholecystitis more often involves sustained pain, localized tenderness, and inflammatory features.
02

Differentiate and manage

  • Ultrasound may show gallstones, gallbladder wall changes and pericholecystic findings.
  • Cholangitis must be considered when biliary symptoms are accompanied by systemic infection and cholestasis.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not use presence of gallstones alone to conclude that every abdominal pain episode is acute cholecystitis.
