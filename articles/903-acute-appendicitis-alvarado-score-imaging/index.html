Acute Appendicitis: Alvarado Score & Imaging

Migratory pain, Rovsing/Psoas signs, and CT findings in pediatric vs adult cases...

Final Year (Part 2) · Surgery

The Modified Alvarado Score (MANTRELS - Total 10 Points):
• M: Migratory right iliac fossa pain (1)
• A: Anorexia (1)
• N: Nausea / vomiting (1)
• T: Tenderness in RIF (2 points)
• R: Rebound tenderness (1)
• E: Elevated temperature >37.3°C (1)
• L: Leukocytosis >10,000 /µL (2 points)
• S: Shift to left of neutrophils (1)
Score ≥ 7: Strongly predictive of acute appendicitis; warrants surgical consultation/appendectomy.

Classic Clinical Signs:
• McBurney's Point: Maximal tenderness at 1/3rd distance from ASIS to umbilicus.
• Rovsing's Sign: Palpation of Left Lower Quadrant causes pain in Right Lower Quadrant.
• Psoas Sign: Pain on passive extension of right hip (indicates retrocecal appendix).
• Obturator Sign: Pain on internal rotation of flexed right hip (indicates pelvic appendix).

Diagnostic Imaging Standards:
• Adults & Elderly: Contrast-enhanced CT Abdomen (Gold Standard: Appendiceal diameter >6 mm, appendicolith, fat stranding).
• Children & Pregnant Females: Graded Compression Ultrasound (non-compressible blind-ended tubular structure >6 mm) or MRI to prevent radiation exposure.
