Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF) Analysis Master Chart

Differentiating Bacterial, Viral, TB, and Fungal meningitis at a glance...

Critical Lab Values · CSF & Fluids

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

  • Normal CSF: Opening pressure 50–200 mmH2O; Cells <5 /µL (all lymphocytes); Protein 15–45 mg/dL; Glucose >60% of blood glucose.
  • Acute Bacterial Meningitis: Markedly elevated pressure (>200); Cells 1,000–10,000 /µL (predominantly Neutrophils); Protein markedly elevated (>100 mg/dL); Glucose markedly reduced (<40% of serum).
  • Viral (Aseptic) Meningitis: Normal or slightly elevated pressure; Cells 50–500 /µL (Lymphocytes); Protein normal or mildly elevated; Glucose completely Normal.
  • Tubercular (TB) Meningitis: Elevated pressure; Cells 100–500 /µL (Lymphocytes); Protein very high (up to 500 mg/dL, forms cobweb coagulum on standing); Glucose very low (<30% of serum).
  • Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS): Albuminocytological Dissociation (very high protein with normal cell count).
