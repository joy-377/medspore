  • Acid–base interpretation requires pH, bicarbonate, respiratory compensation and anion-gap context.

01

Interpret the result in context

  • Lactate can rise with tissue hypoperfusion but also with seizures, beta-agonists, hepatic impairment or other causes.
  • Serial lactate and the overall clinical picture are more informative than labeling every elevated value as septic shock.
02

Clinical interpretation & next steps

  • Acid–base interpretation requires pH, bicarbonate, respiratory compensation and anion-gap context.
  • Reassess perfusion, oxygen delivery, medications and the underlying cause rather than treating a number in isolation.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Lactic acidosis is not synonymous with sepsis, and lactate alone does not diagnose hypoperfusion.
