India National Immunization Schedule: The Logic of Age-Based Prevention

Understand birth doses, infant primary series and boosters while checking the current official Indian UIP schedule.

Quick Revision

  • At birth, the national program includes BCG, oral polio birth dose and hepatitis B birth dose.
  • Primary infant vaccination follows age-appropriate schedules and formulation-specific rules.
  • Catch-up vaccination depends on valid previous doses, age, risk and the latest official guidance.
01

Recognize the schedule structure

  • The routine schedule combines birth, early infant, later infant and childhood booster visits rather than a single uniform interval.
  • JE and certain other vaccines may depend on program or geographical eligibility.
02

Safety and administration

  • Document vaccine, batch, route, site and prior doses; use the exact formulation-specific instructions.
  • Temperature excursions require a documented cold-chain response instead of assuming stock remains potent.
03

Exam trap — do not memorize an outdated chart

  • Schedules, product presentations and state-specific implementation can be revised; check the current Ministry of Health/National Health Mission guidance.
  • A delayed series generally needs an appropriate catch-up plan, not indiscriminate restarting.
