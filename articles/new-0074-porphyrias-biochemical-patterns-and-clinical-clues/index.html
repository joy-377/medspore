Core biomedical mechanism

  • Acute intermittent porphyria can cause recurrent abdominal pain, autonomic symptoms and neuropathy without a characteristic photosensitive rash.
  • Porphyria cutanea tarda is associated with cutaneous photosensitivity and blistering on sun-exposed skin.
