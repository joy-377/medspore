Pathophysiology & identification

  • Proto-oncogenes become oncogenes through gain-of-function changes that promote abnormal growth.
  • Tumor suppressor genes often contribute to cancer through loss of function; the two-hit model is an important conceptual pattern.
02

Diagnostic and treatment connections

  • Examples include RAS as an oncogene and RB1 or TP53 as tumor suppressors.
  • Alterations can be inherited or acquired; molecular context determines therapeutic implications.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Not every tumor suppressor follows a literal two-hit genetic pattern in all tumors.
