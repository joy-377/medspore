Cracking INI-CET Question Patterns

Multiple True/False, Match the following, and Negative Marking caution...

Exam Strategy · INI-CET Strategy

The -0.33 Penalty (33% Negative Marking):
Unlike NEET PG (25% negative marking), INI-CET penalizes incorrect attempts more heavily. Rule of thumb: Attempt questions where you can confidently eliminate at least one distractor; avoid blind guesses when three or four options remain completely unfamiliar.

Multiple True-False & Combined Options:
Treat each statement as an independent true/false query. Write 'T' or 'F' beside each sub-clause before looking at combinations (e.g., '1, 2, and 4 only').

Focus on Core First & Second Year Basics:
AIIMS historically emphasizes basic sciences: Molecular biology, genetic mutations (e.g., RET, MEN syndromes), receptor pharmacology, and electron microscopy findings.
