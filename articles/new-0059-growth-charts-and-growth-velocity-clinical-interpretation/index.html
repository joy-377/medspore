Quick Revision

  • Interpret weight, length or height and head circumference in context of prematurity and family pattern.
  • Incorrect technique or plotting can create false alarm; verify measurements before concluding.

Essential distinctions

  • Growth requires serial measurements with correctly chosen age- and sex-appropriate charts.
  • Growth velocity and crossing centile channels can reveal concerns that one isolated percentile misses.
