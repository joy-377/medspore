Acute Stroke Management & Window Timelines

IV Thrombolysis (rtPA) criteria, mechanical thrombectomy, and BP targets...

Final Year (Part 2) · Medicine

Step 1: Emergency Triage & Neuroimaging
Immediate non-contrast head CT (NCCT) or diffusion-weighted MRI to definitively differentiate Ischemic Stroke from Hemorrhagic Stroke.

Step 2: Intravenous Thrombolysis (IV Alteplase / Tenecteplase):
• Therapeutic Time Window: Within 4.5 hours of symptom onset (or last known normal).
• Blood Pressure Mandate: BP must be < 185/110 mmHg before administering rtPA (treat with IV Labetalol or Nicardipine). Maintain BP < 180/105 mmHg for the next 24 hours.
• Key Absolute Contraindications: Prior ICH at any time, ischemic stroke or head trauma within past 3 months, active internal bleeding, platelet count < 100,000 /µL, INR > 1.7.

Step 3: Endovascular Mechanical Thrombectomy:
• Indicated for Large Vessel Occlusions (LVO) of the anterior circulation (Internal Carotid Artery or M1 segment of Middle Cerebral Artery).
• Can be performed up to 6 to 24 hours from onset in selected patients meeting perfusion mismatch criteria (DAWN and DEFUSE-3 trials).
