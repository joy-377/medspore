Medical & Surgical Stratification: CURB-65 & Child-Pugh

Pneumonia mortality prediction, Cirrhosis grading, and APGAR parameters...

High-Yield Tables · Clinical Scoring Systems

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

CURB-65 Score (Pneumonia Severity):
• C: Confusion (AMTS ≤ 8).
• U: Urea > 7 mmol/L (BUN > 19 mg/dL).
• R: Respiratory rate ≥ 30 /min.
• B: Blood pressure (SBP < 90 or DBP ≤ 60 mmHg).
• 65: Age ≥ 65 years.
Score 0-1: Outpatient; Score 2: Inpatient ward; Score ≥ 3: Intensive Care Unit (ICU).

Child-Pugh Score (Cirrhosis Severity - Parameters: 'Pour Another Beer At Eleven'):
• PT/INR, Ascites, Bilirubin, Albumin, Encephalopathy.
• Class A (5-6 points): Well compensated.
• Class B (7-9 points): Significant functional compromise.
• Class C (10-15 points): Decompensated cirrhosis.

APGAR Score (1 and 5 minutes post-delivery - Max 10):
• Appearance (Color: 0-Blue/pale, 1-Acrocyanotic, 2-Pink).
• Pulse (0-Absent, 1-<100 bpm, 2->100 bpm).
• Grimace (0-No response, 1-Grimace, 2-Cough/sneeze/cry).
• Activity (0-Flaccid, 1-Some flexion, 2-Active motion).
• Respiration (0-Absent, 1-Slow/irregular, 2-Good vigorous cry).
