Renal Function: Serum Creatinine, eGFR & BUN/Cr Ratio

Differentiating Prerenal, Intrinsic, and Postrenal Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)...

Critical Lab Values · Pathology Markers

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

BUN to Serum Creatinine Ratio:
• Ratio > 20:1: Points to Prerenal Azotemia (dehydration, volume depletion, heart failure). Increased urea reabsorption along with sodium/water in proximal tubules.
• Ratio 10:1 to 15:1: Normal or Intrinsic Renal AKI (Acute Tubular Necrosis - ATN). Damaged tubules fail to reabsorb urea.

Fractional Excretion of Sodium (FENa):
FENa = [(Urine Na × Serum Cr) / (Serum Na × Urine Cr)] × 100.
• FENa < 1%: Prerenal Azotemia (kidneys actively conserving sodium). Urine osmolality >500 mOsm/kg, Urine Na < 20 mEq/L.
• FENa > 2%: Intrinsic AKI / ATN (tubular resorption lost). Urine osmolality <350 mOsm/kg, Urine Na > 40 mEq/L, 'Muddy brown granular casts'.
