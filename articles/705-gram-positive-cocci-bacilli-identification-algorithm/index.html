Gram-Positive Cocci & Bacilli Identification Algorithm

Staphylococcus vs Streptococcus, hemolysis, and spore-forming bacilli...

Second Year MBBS · Microbiology

Gram-Positive Cocci Algorithm:
• Catalase Test: Positive = Staphylococcus; Negative = Streptococcus.
• Staphylococci (Coagulase Test):
  - Coagulase Positive: S. aureus (Golden yellow colonies, Protein A virulence factor, causes abscesses, osteomyelitis, endocarditis in IV drug abusers).
  - Coagulase Negative, Novobiocin Sensitive: S. epidermidis (Biofilm producer on prosthetic valves and IV catheters).
  - Coagulase Negative, Novobiocin Resistant: S. saprophyticus (UTI in young sexually active females).
• Streptococci (Hemolysis on Blood Agar):
  - Alpha-Hemolytic (Partial greening): S. pneumoniae (Optochin sensitive, bile soluble, lancet-shaped diplococci, capsule quellung positive); Viridans Streptococci (Optochin resistant, dental caries, subacute bacterial endocarditis).
  - Beta-Hemolytic (Complete clearing): S. pyogenes (GAS) (Bacitracin sensitive, PYR positive, causes rheumatic fever and PSGN); S. agalactiae (GBS) (Bacitracin resistant, CAMP test positive, neonatal sepsis/meningitis).

Gram-Positive Bacilli:
• Spore-Forming: Bacillus anthracis (Medusa head colonies, poly-D-glutamic acid capsule); Clostridium species (Obligate anaerobes — C. tetani, C. botulinum, C. perfringens, C. difficile).
• Non-Spore Forming: Corynebacterium diphtheriae (Chinese letter pattern on Gram stain, Albert stain reveals metachromatic volutin granules, black colonies on Tellurite agar); Listeria monocytogenes (Tumbling motility at 25°C, causes meningitis in neonates and elderly).
