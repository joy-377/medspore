High-Yield Eponymous Nodes & Spots

Roth spots, Osler nodes, Janeway lesions, and Koplik spots...

Triads & Signs · Eponyms

  • Infective Endocarditis Stigmata:
    1. Osler Nodes: Painful, tender, raised nodules on finger/toe pads (Immune complex deposition).
    2. Janeway Lesions: Painless, erythematous macules on palms and soles (Microemboli).
    3. Roth Spots: Exudative, edematous hemorrhagic lesions of the retina with pale centers.
  • Koplik Spots: Tiny white/blue spots on an erythematous base on the buccal mucosa opposite the molars. Pathognomonic for: Measles (appears before the rash).
  • Virchow's Node: Enlarged, hard, non-tender left supraclavicular lymph node. Signifies: Metastatic abdominal malignancy (Gastric Cancer, known as Troisier's sign).
  • Sister Mary Joseph Nodule: Palpable nodule bulging into the umbilicus. Signifies: Advanced intra-abdominal malignancy (Gastric/Ovarian).
