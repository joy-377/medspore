Quick Revision

  • Both require emergency care and treatment directed to physiology and precipitating factors.
  • Biochemical results support diagnosis, but treatment decisions in unstable patients are driven by the syndrome.
01

Clinical syndrome and red flags

  • Thyroid storm is life-threatening thyrotoxicosis with severe systemic decompensation such as hyperthermia, tachyarrhythmia or delirium.
  • Myxedema coma is severe hypothyroidism with hypothermia, altered mental status, hypoventilation or circulatory compromise.
02

Differentiate and manage

  • Both require emergency care and treatment directed to physiology and precipitating factors.
  • Biochemical results support diagnosis, but treatment decisions in unstable patients are driven by the syndrome.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not confuse the two because both can cause altered consciousness; temperature and cardiovascular findings differ.
