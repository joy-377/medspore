Pacing & Fighting Decision Fatigue During Mock Tests

The 45-second rule, cognitive reset drills, and mid-exam pacing...

Exam Strategy · Time Management

The 45-Second Rule:
Every question carries the same weight. Spending 3 minutes on a difficult question sacrifices time needed for 3 easy recall questions at the end of the paper. If a question is unclear after 45 seconds, make an educated guess, flag it, and advance.

Combating Decision Fatigue (The 60-Question Cognitive Reset):
Around questions 60–80 and 140–160, attention spans drop. Take a mandatory 10-second micro-pause: Close your eyes, sit upright, take two deep diaphragmatic breaths, blink rapidly to rehydrate eyes, and resume.

Simulate Real Exam Conditions:
Take grand mocks during the exact exam hours (9:00 AM to 12:30 PM). Train your body's circadian rhythm to avoid heavy meals and sluggishness during morning hours.
