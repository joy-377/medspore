Recognize the question type

  • Pathology gives the structural or molecular lesion; pharmacology links that lesion to a therapeutic target.
  • Identify the relevant receptor, enzyme, pathway or cell before choosing a drug effect.

Use this in timed practice

03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Avoid treating every drug in one class as identical in selectivity or adverse-effect profile.
