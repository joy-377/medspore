01

Recognize the pattern

  • Reynolds pentad adds hypotension and altered mental status to Charcot triad of fever, jaundice and right upper quadrant pain.
  • The pentad suggests severe cholangitis with circulatory or neurologic involvement.
02

What this finding does—and does not—mean

  • Sepsis resuscitation, source control and urgent evaluation for biliary drainage are essential principles.
  • Elderly or immunocompromised patients may show nonspecific presentations rather than the complete eponym.
03

Memory aid

  • Reynolds pentad: Charcot triad plus hypotension and altered mental status.
  • It signals severe disease, but waiting for all five findings would be unsafe.
04

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not wait for all five features before escalating a patient with suspected severe biliary sepsis.
