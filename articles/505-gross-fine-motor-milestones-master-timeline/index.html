High-Yield Tables · Developmental Milestones

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

Gross Motor Milestones:
• 3 months: Neck holding.
• 5 months: Rolls over (tummy to back).
• 6 months: Sits with support (tripod fashion).
• 8 months: Sits steadily without support.
• 9 months: Stands with support; crawls.
• 10 months: Cruising (walks holding onto furniture).
• 12 months: Walks independently without support.
• 18 months: Runs stiffly, kicks a ball.
• 2 years: Climbs stairs (2 feet per step).
• 3 years: Climbs stairs (alternating feet), rides a tricycle.
• 4 years: Hops on one foot.

Fine Motor Milestones:
• 4 months: Bidextrous reach (reaches with both hands).
• 6 months: Unidextrous reach (transfers objects hand-to-hand).
• 9 months: Immature pincer grasp.
• 12 months: Mature pincer grasp (picks pellet with thumb and index).
• Block Towers: 15 mo (2 cubes), 18 mo (3 cubes), 2 yr (6 cubes), 3 yr (9 cubes).
• Copying Shapes: 18 mo (scribbles), 3 yr (circle), 4 yr (cross and square), 5 yr (triangle).
