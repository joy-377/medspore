Prescribing in Pregnancy: Safety Principles

Which antihypertensives, antibiotics, and analgesics are safe for expecting mothers...

Clinical Tips · Prescription Pearls

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

Clinical principle: Medication safety in pregnancy depends on gestational age, maternal condition, dose, route, and the best available evidence. The former FDA A/B/C/D/X letter categories have been replaced by narrative pregnancy and lactation labeling.

Practical approach

  • Confirm the indication and gestational age before choosing a drug.
  • Use current, medicine-specific prescribing guidance and local obstetric protocols.
  • Review fetal and maternal risks, effective alternatives, and the risks of untreated disease.
  • Avoid assuming that every agent within a drug class shares an identical risk profile.

For real patients, consult up-to-date institutional guidance and a qualified clinician.
