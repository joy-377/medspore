Weightage & Subject Sequencing Strategy

High-yield vs short subjects: Allocating revision time for maximum ROI...

Exam Strategy · NEET PG Strategy

The High-Yield Clinical Core (50%+ of the paper):
Medicine, Surgery, OBG, Pathology, and Pharmacology form the foundation. Clinical vignettes primarily integrate these core subjects.

The High-Yield 'Short Subjects' (Maximum Return on Investment):
Radiology, Dermatology, Psychiatry, Anesthesia, and Orthopedics contribute nearly 45–50 questions collectively. Their syllabi are concise, and the questions are largely repetitive. Mastering short subjects gives a reliable boost in overall percentiles.

First-Year Subjects (The Differentiator):
Anatomy (focus heavily on nerve lesions and cross-sectional imaging), Physiology (acid-base and cardiac cycles), and Biochemistry (inborn errors of metabolism and vitamin deficiencies) often determine top ranks.
