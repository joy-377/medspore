Quick Revision

  • Fetal hemoglobin and carbon monoxide exposure alter oxygen binding behavior in clinically different ways.
  • Pulse oximetry alone does not fully describe oxygen content or tissue delivery.
01

Core biomedical mechanism

  • A right shift of the oxygen–hemoglobin dissociation curve favors oxygen unloading to tissues.
  • Increased carbon dioxide, higher temperature, lower pH and increased 2,3-BPG commonly shift the curve right.
02

Clinical application

  • Fetal hemoglobin and carbon monoxide exposure alter oxygen binding behavior in clinically different ways.
  • Pulse oximetry alone does not fully describe oxygen content or tissue delivery.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • A higher hemoglobin saturation at a given partial pressure does not automatically mean better tissue oxygen delivery.
