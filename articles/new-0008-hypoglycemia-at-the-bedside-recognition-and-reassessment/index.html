01

Recognize the situation

  • Hypoglycemia may produce sweating, tremor, hunger, confusion, seizure or reduced consciousness.
  • Confirm glucose promptly and provide appropriate glucose treatment using a safe route based on consciousness and swallowing ability.
02

Clinical decisions & differential

  • Recheck glucose and assess recurrence risk, especially with long-acting insulin or sulfonylureas.
  • Investigate missed meals, renal impairment, medication mismatch and acute illness after stabilizing the patient.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not give oral intake to a patient unable to protect their airway.
