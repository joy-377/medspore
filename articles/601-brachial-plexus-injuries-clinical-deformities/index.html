Quick Revision

  • Erb-Duchenne Palsy (Upper Trunk Lesion - C5, C6); Mechanism: Excessive increase in angle between head and shoulder (birth trauma, fall on shoulder).
  • Klumpke's Palsy (Lower Trunk Lesion - C8, T1); Mechanism: Upward traction on the arm (breech delivery, clutching a tree branch while falling).
01

Erb-Duchenne Palsy (Upper Trunk Lesion - C5, C6)

Erb-Duchenne Palsy (Upper Trunk Lesion - C5, C6):
• Mechanism: Excessive increase in angle between head and shoulder (birth trauma, fall on shoulder).
• Nerves Involved: Suprascapular, Axillary, Musculocutaneous.
• Muscles Paralyzed: Supraspinatus, Infraspinatus, Deltoid, Biceps brachii, Brachialis, Supinator.
• Deformity: Policeman's tip / Waiter's tip deformity (Arm adducted and medially rotated, forearm extended and pronated).
• Sensory Loss: Outer aspect of arm and forearm.

02

Klumpke's Palsy (Lower Trunk Lesion - C8, T1)

Klumpke's Palsy (Lower Trunk Lesion - C8, T1):
• Mechanism: Upward traction on the arm (breech delivery, clutching a tree branch while falling).
• Nerves Involved: Ulnar nerve mainly, Median nerve partially.
• Muscles Paralyzed: All intrinsic muscles of the hand (interossei, lumbricals).
• Deformity: Complete Claw Hand (Hyperextension at MCP joints, flexion at IP joints).
• Associated Feature: Horner's Syndrome (Ptosis, Miosis, Anhidrosis) due to sympathetic T1 root involvement.
