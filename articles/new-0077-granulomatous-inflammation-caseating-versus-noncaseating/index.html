01

Pathophysiology & identification

  • Granulomas are organized collections of activated macrophages that may form in chronic inflammatory conditions.
  • Caseating granulomas are associated with tuberculosis and some fungal infections; noncaseating granulomas occur in sarcoidosis and other conditions.
02

Diagnostic and treatment connections

  • Histologic appearance alone is insufficient to confirm organism or cause.
  • Interpret biopsy alongside microbiology, exposure and the distribution of disease.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Not every noncaseating granuloma indicates sarcoidosis, and not every tuberculous granuloma shows classic caseation.
