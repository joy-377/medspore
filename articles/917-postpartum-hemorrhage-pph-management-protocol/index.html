Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH) Management Protocol

The 4 Ts (Tone, Tissue, Trauma, Thrombin), Bakri balloon, and B-Lynch suture...

Final Year (Part 2) · OBG

The 4 Ts of PPH (Etiology Breakdown):
• Tone (70%): Uterine atony (most common cause). Risk factors: Polyhydramnios, multiple gestation, prolonged labor.
• Tissue (20%): Retained placental tissue or membranes.
• Trauma (10%): Cervical/vaginal lacerations, uterine rupture, or uterine inversion.
• Thrombin (<1%): Pre-existing or acquired coagulopathy (HELLP, DIC, von Willebrand disease).

Stepwise Surgical & Medical Algorithm:
• Step 1: Bimanual uterine massage + IV Oxytocin infusion (20-40 IU in 1000 mL crystalloid) + Methylergonovine (contraindicated in chronic hypertension) or Carboprost (contraindicated in asthma) / Misoprostol.
• Step 2: Uterine balloon tamponade (Bakri balloon or condom catheter filled with 300-500 mL saline).
• Step 3: Surgical interventions — B-Lynch compression suture (for atony), Uterine/Internal Iliac artery ligation.
• Step 4: Subtotal or Total Peripartum Hysterectomy (the definitive life-saving last resort when bleeding is uncontrollable).
