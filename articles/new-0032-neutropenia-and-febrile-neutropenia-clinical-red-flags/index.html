Quick Revision

  • Prompt assessment, cultures where possible and empiric antimicrobials according to institutional risk pathways are important.
  • Consider chemotherapy, marrow disease, drugs and infection as potential causes of neutropenia.
01

Interpret the result in context

  • Neutropenia increases infection risk; fever during severe neutropenia can indicate a medical emergency.
  • Symptoms of infection may be muted because the inflammatory response is reduced.
02

Clinical interpretation & next steps

  • Prompt assessment, cultures where possible and empiric antimicrobials according to institutional risk pathways are important.
  • Consider chemotherapy, marrow disease, drugs and infection as potential causes of neutropenia.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not dismiss fever in a neutropenic patient because the examination lacks pus or marked redness.
