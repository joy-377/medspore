Recognize the situation

  • New confusion, increasing respiratory rate, hypotension and falling urine output can be early deterioration signals.
  • An early-warning score organizes observations but does not replace clinical judgment or escalation when staff are concerned.
02

Clinical decisions & differential

  • Recheck observations, glucose, oxygenation, medications and likely triggers while activating a rapid response pathway as indicated.
  • Trend matters: a progressive change may be more informative than one isolated measurement.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not dismiss a patient's worsening status because a single score is below an alert threshold.
