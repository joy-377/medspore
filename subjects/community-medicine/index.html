Community Medicine — Medical MCQs

Practise 10 sample questions across 8 subject topic groups. Each MCQ includes explanation and incorrect-option analysis.

Topic outline

  • Concepts of Health & Disease: Iceberg Phenomenon of Disease, Levels of Prevention, Natural History of Disease, Multifactorial Causation and Web of Causation, Modes of Intervention and Health Promotion
  • Epidemiology & Screening: Sensitivity and Specificity, Predictive Values and Disease Prevalence, Lead-Time Bias, Study Designs: Cohort and Case-Control Studies, Odds Ratio in Case-Control Studies
  • Biostatistics: Measures of Central Tendency, Standard Deviation and Standard Error, Hypothesis Testing and P Values, Type I and Type II Errors, Choosing Statistical Tests
  • Communicable Diseases: Tuberculosis: Diagnosis and Public Health, Tuberculosis: Contact Investigation and Preventive Treatment, Leprosy: Contact Management, Malaria: Vector Control, Dengue: Vector Ecology and Prevention
  • Non-Communicable Diseases: Hypertension Screening and Control, Diabetes Mellitus: Screening and Prevention, Cancer Screening: Cervical Cancer, Tobacco Control and Brief Intervention, Cardiovascular Risk Assessment
  • Demography & Family Planning: Demographic Transition, Crude Birth Rate and General Fertility Rate, Contraception: Intrauterine Devices, Contraception: Emergency Contraception, Couple Protection Rate and Unmet Need
  • Nutrition & Environment: Protein-Energy Malnutrition, Vitamin A Deficiency, Iodine Deficiency Disorders, Water Purification and Chlorination, Air Pollution and Particulate Matter
  • National Health Programs: National Tuberculosis Elimination Programme, National AIDS Control Programme, Universal Immunization Programme, Mission Indradhanush, National Programme for Prevention and Control of Non-Communicable Diseases
