01

Recognize the pattern

  • Acute limb ischemia is classically described by pain, pallor, pulselessness, paresthesia, paralysis and poikilothermia.
  • A threatened limb needs urgent vascular assessment; sensory or motor deficits indicate substantial ischemic risk.
02

What this finding does—and does not—mean

  • Compare both limbs for temperature, color, capillary refill, pulses and Doppler signals.
  • Rapid assessment and appropriate anticoagulation or reperfusion planning depend on specialist evaluation.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not wait for all six signs; pulselessness may be difficult to assess and tissue damage is time-sensitive.
