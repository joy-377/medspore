01

Cardiovascular Emergencies

Cardiovascular Emergencies:
• Paroxysmal Supraventricular Tachycardia (PSVT): IV Adenosine (rapid bolus 6 mg → 12 mg).
• Atrial Fibrillation with WPW Syndrome: IV Procainamide or Ibutilide (AV-nodal blockers like Verapamil/Digoxin are contraindicated).
• Torsades de Pointes: IV Magnesium Sulfate (even if serum magnesium is normal).
• Anaphylactic Shock: IM Adrenaline (Epinephrine 1:1000, 0.5 mg in anterolateral thigh).

02

Endocrine Emergencies

Endocrine Emergencies:
• Pheochromocytoma (Pre-operative BP control): Phenoxybenzamine (alpha-blocker first; beta-blocker added later to avoid hypertensive crisis).
• Thyroid Storm: Propylthiouracil (PTU - blocks synthesis & peripheral T4 to T3 conversion) + Lugol's Iodine + Beta-blocker (Propranolol) + Hydrocortisone.
• Myxedema Coma: IV Levothyroxine (T4) + IV Hydrocortisone (to prevent acute adrenal insufficiency).
• Central Diabetes Insipidus: Desmopressin (DDAVP).
