Interpret the result in context

  • CSF opening pressure is measured during lumbar puncture under standardized patient positioning and technique.
  • Body position, straining, anxiety, sedation and the clinical situation can affect pressure interpretation.
02

Clinical interpretation & next steps

  • Evaluate pressure alongside CSF cells, glucose, protein and appropriate microbiology tests.
  • A normal CSF opening pressure does not exclude every neurological infection or inflammatory disorder.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Interpret pressure in context rather than treating one measurement as a stand-alone diagnosis.
