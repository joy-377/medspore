01

Clinical syndrome and red flags

  • Triple assessment of a breast lump integrates clinical examination, appropriate imaging and tissue diagnosis when indicated.
  • Imaging is chosen by patient age, presentation and local guidelines rather than one universal modality.
02

Differentiate and manage

  • A suspicious palpable lump may need biopsy even when one imaging test is reassuring.
  • Assessment includes skin, nipple changes, nodes and family or personal risk history.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not label a persistent suspicious lump benign solely from a normal single test.
