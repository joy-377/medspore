Approach to Upper Gastrointestinal Bleeding (UGIB)

Glasgow-Blatchford score, IV Pantoprazole, variceal banding, and terlipressin...

Final Year (Part 2) · Medicine

Immediate Resuscitation & Stabilization:
• Two large-bore IV cannulas, aggressive fluid resuscitation, and blood transfusion (maintain Hb > 7 g/dL, or > 9 g/dL in coronary artery disease).
• Correct coagulopathy with Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP) or platelets if INR > 1.5 or platelets < 50,000.
• High-dose PPI Infusion: IV Pantoprazole bolus followed by continuous infusion (8 mg/hr) for non-variceal bleeds.

Variceal Bleeding Specifics:
• If variceal bleed is suspected (e.g., chronic liver disease / ascites): Start Vasoactive Drugs (Terlipressin, Octreotide, or Somatostatin) immediately to lower portal pressure.
• Administer prophylactic antibiotics (Ceftriaxone or Norfloxacin) to prevent spontaneous bacterial peritonitis / sepsis.
• Perform diagnostic and therapeutic Upper GI Endoscopy (UGIE) within 12 hours for variceal band ligation (VBL) or sclerotherapy.
