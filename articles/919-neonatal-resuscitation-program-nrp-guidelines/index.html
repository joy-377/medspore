Neonatal Resuscitation Program (NRP) Guidelines

Initial steps, golden minute, heart rate evaluation, and positive pressure ventilation...

Final Year (Part 2) · Pediatrics

The 'Golden Minute' of Newborn Resuscitation:
Initial steps must be completed within the first 60 seconds of life: Provide warmth, position airway (sniffing position), clear secretions if needed, dry, stimulate, and assess.

Heart Rate (HR) Decisive Algorithm:
• HR > 100 bpm with labored breathing / cyanosis: Clear airway, monitor SpO2, consider CPAP.
• HR between 60 to 100 bpm (or gasping/apnea): Initiate Positive Pressure Ventilation (PPV) immediately at 40-60 breaths/min. Monitor SpO2 on right hand (pre-ductal).
• HR < 60 bpm despite effective PPV for 30 seconds:
  - 1. Intubate trachea (or laryngeal mask airway).
  - 2. Initiate Chest Compressions coordinated with PPV at a 3:1 ratio (90 compressions + 30 breaths per minute).
  - 3. Administer IV Epinephrine (0.01-0.03 mg/kg of 1:10,000 concentration via umbilical venous catheter) if HR remains < 60 bpm despite chest compressions and effective ventilation.
