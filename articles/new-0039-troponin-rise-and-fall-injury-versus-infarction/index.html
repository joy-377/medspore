Interpret the result in context

  • Troponin elevation indicates myocardial injury, but myocardial infarction requires evidence of acute ischemia in the appropriate context.
  • A dynamic rise or fall supports acute injury, whereas chronic elevations may occur in kidney disease or structural heart disease.
02

Clinical interpretation & next steps

  • ECG findings, symptoms, imaging and serial results help classify ischemic and nonischemic causes.
  • Sepsis, myocarditis and pulmonary embolism can also produce myocardial injury.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • A single elevated troponin result is not equivalent to a confirmed type 1 myocardial infarction.
