Essential distinctions

  • Beta-lactams inhibit bacterial cell-wall synthesis; macrolides act at the 50S ribosomal subunit; aminoglycosides act at 30S.
  • Mechanism and spectrum must be considered alongside local susceptibility and patient-specific factors.
