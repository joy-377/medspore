Cardiac Biomarkers: Kinetics & Relapse Detection

Interpret cardiac troponin using serial measurements, a validated assay and the clinical/ECG picture, rather than a universal timing chart.

Quick Revision

  • Myocardial injury is defined by elevated cardiac troponin; acute injury is suggested by a rise or fall on serial testing.
  • Myocardial infarction additionally requires evidence of acute myocardial ischemia; troponin elevation alone does not establish an infarction.
  • High-sensitivity assays have laboratory-specific thresholds and testing intervals; do not apply a universal cutoff.
01

Troponin: injury is not always infarction

  • Consider symptoms, electrocardiographic evidence, imaging and other causes of troponin elevation.
  • Myocarditis, renal dysfunction, severe illness and tachyarrhythmias can accompany troponin elevation without plaque rupture.
02

Use assay-specific serial testing

  • Interpret baseline and repeat high-sensitivity troponin under the validated local diagnostic pathway.
  • Recurrent symptoms after a recent infarction require clinical assessment, repeat ECG and selected serial biomarkers, not a single rigid timeline.
03

Exam trap

  • A normal early measurement does not always exclude an acute evolving coronary syndrome.
  • CK-MB is not generally the preferred modern biomarker when validated cardiac troponin testing is available.
