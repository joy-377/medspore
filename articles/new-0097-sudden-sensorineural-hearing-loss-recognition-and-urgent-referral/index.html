Recognize the core pattern

  • Sudden sensorineural hearing loss is an otologic urgency and may be mistaken for earwax or middle-ear disease.
  • Use otoscopy and bedside Weber/Rinne assessment to help distinguish conductive from sensorineural impairment.
02

Clinical examination and interpretation

  • Prompt audiometry and specialist assessment improve diagnostic and treatment decision-making.
  • Consider associated neurologic signs that might indicate a central cause.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not delay referral for sudden unexplained hearing loss with a normal-looking ear canal.
