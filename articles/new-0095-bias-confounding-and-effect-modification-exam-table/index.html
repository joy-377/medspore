Recognize the core pattern

  • Selection bias arises from how participants are sampled or retained; information bias arises from measurement errors.
  • Confounding occurs when another variable distorts an exposure-outcome association.
02

Clinical examination and interpretation

  • Effect modification occurs when the effect genuinely differs across levels of another variable.
  • Randomization, design restrictions, stratification and multivariable adjustment address different threats to inference.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Effect modification should be described rather than simply adjusted away as if always an error.
