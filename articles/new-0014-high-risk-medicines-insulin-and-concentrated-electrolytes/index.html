  • Separate concentrated electrolytes from routine ward stock or restrict access according to the safety system.

01

Recognize the situation

  • Insulin and concentrated potassium are high-alert medicines because dosing or administration errors can cause rapid harm.
  • Use standardized concentrations, clear units, independent checks when policy requires and appropriate administration devices.
02

Clinical decisions & differential

  • Separate concentrated electrolytes from routine ward stock or restrict access according to the safety system.
  • Monitor glucose and potassium after high-risk administration; investigate mismatches between prescription and administration records.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Insulin units are not milliliters; ambiguous abbreviations can lead to serious errors.
