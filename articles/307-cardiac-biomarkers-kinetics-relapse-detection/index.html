Cardiac Biomarkers: Kinetics & Relapse Detection

Troponin I vs CK-MB timelines and ruling out early re-infarction...

Critical Lab Values · Pathology Markers

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

Cardiac Troponin I & T (Gold Standard for Myocardial Infarction):
• Initial rise: 3–4 hours.
• Peak: 12–24 hours.
• Duration: Remains elevated for 7–14 days.
• Limitation: Because it stays elevated for up to two weeks, it cannot reliably detect an early re-infarction occurring within a few days of the initial event.

CK-MB (Creatine Kinase-MB):
• Initial rise: 4–6 hours.
• Peak: 18–24 hours.
• Duration: Returns to baseline within 48–72 hours (2–3 days).
• Clinical Utility: The marker of choice to diagnose Re-infarction occurring after 3 days of index MI.

Myoglobin: Rises earliest (1–2 hours), peaks at 6–8 hours, but lacks cardiac specificity.
