Interpret the result in context

  • Serum sodium reflects water balance relative to solute, so the cause of dysnatremia matters as much as the number.
  • Rapid correction of chronic hyponatremia risks osmotic demyelination, especially in vulnerable patients.
02

Clinical interpretation & next steps

  • Assess symptoms, serum osmolality, volume status and medications before selecting correction strategies.
  • Severe neurologic symptoms require urgent supervised treatment and repeated sodium measurements.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not apply a single unmonitored correction plan to every patient, particularly when chronicity is uncertain.
