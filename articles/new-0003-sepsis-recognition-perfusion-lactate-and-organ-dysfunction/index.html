Quick Revision

  • Elevated lactate can support concern for impaired perfusion but also has nonhypoxic causes; it is not a stand-alone sepsis test.
  • Early recognition, cultures when feasible without delaying care, source control and appropriate antimicrobials are core principles.
01

Recognize the situation

  • Sepsis is life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated response to infection, not simply a positive blood culture.
  • Look for altered mentation, low blood pressure, reduced urine output, respiratory distress and poor perfusion.
02

Clinical decisions & differential

  • Elevated lactate can support concern for impaired perfusion but also has nonhypoxic causes; it is not a stand-alone sepsis test.
  • Early recognition, cultures when feasible without delaying care, source control and appropriate antimicrobials are core principles.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not wait for fever or a positive culture before recognizing possible sepsis.
