Quick Revision

  • Echocardiography helps classify EF, structural disease and filling abnormalities.
  • Treatment strategies differ by phenotype, congestion, cause and comorbidities; diagnosis is not based on EF alone.
01

Clinical syndrome and red flags

  • Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction involves systolic pump impairment; preserved ejection fraction involves elevated filling pressures despite non-reduced EF.
  • Symptoms can include dyspnea, orthopnea, edema and exercise intolerance across phenotypes.
02

Differentiate and manage

  • Echocardiography helps classify EF, structural disease and filling abnormalities.
  • Treatment strategies differ by phenotype, congestion, cause and comorbidities; diagnosis is not based on EF alone.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • A normal-range EF does not exclude clinical heart failure.
