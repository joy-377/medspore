Managing the Final 30 Days Before the Exam

The 20th notebook revision, avoiding new sources, and active testing...

Exam Strategy · Time Management

1. Freeze Your Resources:
Do not start new textbooks, coaching notes, or lecture videos in the last month. Trust your primary resource and revise only what has already been marked.

2. Master the '20th Notebook' (High-Yield Volatile Sheet):
Dedicate the last 7 days exclusively to volatile memory items: Developmental milestones, IPC sections, biostatistical formulas, chromosome associations, and named signs.

3. Taper Mock Tests:
Stop taking full grand mocks 5–7 days before the main exam. A difficult mock just before the exam can damage confidence. Shift entirely to revision and previous year questions (PYQs).
