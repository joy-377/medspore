Quick Revision

  • Sepsis identification rests on suspected infection with acute organ dysfunction rather than an isolated score.
  • Trending organ function after treatment can help assess illness trajectory.
01

Essential distinctions

  • SOFA summarizes organ dysfunction across respiratory, coagulation, liver, cardiovascular, neurologic and renal systems.
  • qSOFA is a bedside risk prompt, not a sufficiently sensitive stand-alone sepsis screening or diagnostic test.
