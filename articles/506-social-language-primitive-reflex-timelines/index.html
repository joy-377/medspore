Social, Language & Primitive Reflex Timelines

Social smile, stranger anxiety, Moro reflex, and Babinski disappearance...

High-Yield Tables · Developmental Milestones

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

Social & Adaptive Milestones:
• 2 months: Social smile (responds to mother's face).
• 3 months: Recognizes mother.
• 6 months: Recognizes strangers, exhibits stranger anxiety.
• 9 months: Waves 'bye-bye', plays peek-a-boo.
• 12 months: Drinks from a cup, helps in dressing.
• 2 years: Dry by day, tells toilet needs.
• 3 years: Parallel play (plays alongside peers).
• 4 years: Cooperative play (plays with other children).

Language Milestones:
• 3 months: Cooing.
• 6 months: Monosyllables ('ba', 'da').
• 9 months: Bisyllables ('mama', 'dada' non-specific).
• 12 months: 1-2 words with meaning.
• 18 months: 8-10 words vocabulary.
• 2 years: 2-word sentences ('want milk').
• 3 years: Asks questions, knows full name and gender.

Primitive Reflexes (Disappearance Timeline):
• Moro Reflex: Disappears by 4-6 months (persistence implies cerebral palsy).
• Palmar Grasp: Disappears by 3-4 months.
• Rooting Reflex: Disappears by 3-4 months.
• Babinski Sign: Normal in infants; becomes flexor (plantar) by 12-18 months with myelination.
