Abdominal & Neuro Radiology Pearls

Coffee bean sign, String sign, Apple-core lesion, and neuro imaging signs...

Triads & Signs · Radiological Signs

  • Coffee Bean Sign (Abdominal X-Ray): Massively dilated loop of colon appearing like a coffee bean pointing towards the Right Upper Quadrant. Signifies: Sigmoid Volvulus.
  • String Sign of Kantor (Barium Enema): Severe narrowing of the terminal ileum. Signifies: Crohn's Disease.
  • Apple-Core Lesion (Barium Enema): Circumferential narrowing of the colon lumen. Signifies: Colorectal Carcinoma.
  • Bird's Beak Sign (Barium Swallow): Dilated esophagus tapering to a smooth point at the GE junction. Signifies: Achalasia Cardia.
  • Lentiform (Lens) Shape on Head CT: Biconvex hyperdensity that does not cross suture lines. Signifies: Epidural Hematoma (Middle Meningeal Artery rupture).
  • Crescent Shape on Head CT: Concave hyperdensity that CAN cross suture lines. Signifies: Subdural Hematoma (Bridging veins rupture).
