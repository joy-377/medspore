Clinical syndrome and red flags

  • A difficult airway plan prioritizes oxygenation, patient preparation, operator skill and backup strategies.
  • Assessment includes mouth opening, neck mobility, airway pathology, physiology and aspiration risk.
02

Differentiate and manage

  • Failed intubation pathways require clear limits on repeated attempts and timely rescue ventilation.
  • Team communication, equipment readiness and escalation pathways are as important as a single device choice.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Successful tracheal tube placement is not the only goal; maintaining oxygenation is central.
