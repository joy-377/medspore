Weightage & Subject Sequencing Strategy

Plan subject sequencing around deficits and exam utility rather than an unverifiable fixed weightage percentage.

Quick Revision

  • Identify personal weak topics using recent mock results and missed-question patterns.
  • Allocate time based on the importance of each topic, learning gap and days available.
  • Use spaced active recall and mixed-case practice rather than only rereading notes.
01

Prioritize what matters

  • Group mistakes by genuine knowledge gap, interpretation error and exam pacing problem.
  • Spend more time on weaknesses with high clinical or examination relevance while maintaining broad coverage.
02

Measure progress

  • Review the trend in accuracy and speed on unseen questions rather than only familiar flashcards.
  • Use shorter frequent reviews of forgotten material across the revision period.
03

Exam trap

  • No single fixed subject-weightage percentage applies reliably to every future examination.
  • More study hours are not useful if they do not improve retention or transfer to questions.
