Quick Revision

  • ACE inhibitors may cause cough and angioedema; both ACE inhibitors and ARBs require potassium and kidney-function consideration.
  • Selection depends on age, pregnancy, kidney disease, coronary disease and other patient characteristics.
01

Essential distinctions

  • ACE inhibitors and ARBs reduce renin–angiotensin signaling; dihydropyridine calcium blockers predominantly relax vascular smooth muscle.
  • Thiazide-type diuretics, calcium-channel blockers and renin–angiotensin blockers are common first-line classes in appropriate patients.
