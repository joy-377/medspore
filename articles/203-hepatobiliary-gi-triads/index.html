Triads & Signs · Surgical Triads

  • Charcot's Triad (Acute Cholangitis): 1. Right Upper Quadrant (RUQ) pain, 2. Jaundice, 3. Fever with chills/rigors. Next step: ERCP.
  • Reynolds' Pentad (Severe Suppurative Cholangitis): Charcot's Triad + 4. Shock (hypotension), 5. Altered mental status. This is a surgical emergency requiring urgent biliary decompression.
  • Borchardt's Triad (Acute Gastric Volvulus): 1. Severe epigastric pain, 2. Retching without vomiting, 3. Inability to pass a Nasogastric (NG) tube.
  • Mackler's Triad (Boerhaave Syndrome): 1. Vomiting, 2. Lower chest pain, 3. Subcutaneous emphysema (crepitus in the neck). Diagnosis: Esophageal rupture.
