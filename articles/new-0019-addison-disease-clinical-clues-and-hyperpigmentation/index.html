01

Recognize the pattern

  • Primary adrenal insufficiency may produce fatigue, weight loss, postural symptoms, hyponatremia and increased pigmentation.
  • Hyperpigmentation is related to elevated ACTH in primary rather than typical secondary adrenal failure.
02

What this finding does—and does not—mean

  • Hyperkalemia and salt craving can reflect mineralocorticoid deficiency; acute adrenal crisis may feature shock.
  • Diagnosis uses appropriate cortisol and ACTH assessment, often with stimulation testing in a suitable setting.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • A hypotensive patient with suspected adrenal crisis needs urgent clinical management, not delayed care while awaiting tests.
