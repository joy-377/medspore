Forensic Medicine — Medical MCQs

Practise 10 questions across 6 subject topic groups. Each MCQ includes explanation and incorrect-option analysis.

Topic outline

  • Medical Jurisprudence: Consent for Medical Examination and Treatment, Privileged Communication, Professional Negligence and the Standard of Care, Professional Misconduct and Ethical Responsibilities, Medical Certification of Cause of Death
  • Thanatology: Postmortem Changes: Rigor Mortis, Postmortem Lividity, Algor Mortis and Estimation of Time Since Death, Putrefaction and Its Stages, Adipocere Formation
  • Traumatology: Abrasion and Its Types, Contusions and Bruises, Lacerations and Tissue Bridging, Incised Wounds and Hesitation Marks, Defense Wounds
  • Asphyxial Deaths: Hanging: Mechanism and Findings, Ligature Strangulation, Manual Strangulation, Drowning and Diatom Analysis, Smothering and Suffocation
  • Sexual Jurisprudence: Sexual Assault: Medical Examination and Consent, Sexual Assault: Interpretation of Genital Findings, Sexual Assault: Forensic Evidence and Chain of Custody, Sexual Assault: Interpretation of Semen and DNA Evidence, Age Estimation in Medicolegal Cases
  • Toxicology: Organophosphorus Poisoning, Atropine in Organophosphorus Poisoning, Carbon Monoxide Poisoning, Methanol Poisoning, Ethylene Glycol Poisoning
