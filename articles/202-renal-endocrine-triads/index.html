HUS, Whippleundefineds Triad, and the classic features of Pheochromocytoma...

Triads & Signs · Medical Triads

  • Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (HUS): 1. Microangiopathic Hemolytic Anemia (MAHA - schistocytes on smear), 2. Thrombocytopenia, 3. Acute Kidney Injury (AKI). Classic scenario: Child with bloody diarrhea (E. coli O157:H7) developing oliguria.
  • Whipple's Triad (Insulinoma): 1. Symptoms of hypoglycemia (sweating, tremors), 2. Documented low blood glucose (<50 mg/dL), 3. Immediate relief of symptoms after administering IV glucose.
  • Classic Pheochromocytoma Triad: 1. Episodic headache, 2. Sweating (diaphoresis), 3. Tachycardia/Palpitations (often with resistant hypertension).
  • Meltzer's Triad (Cryoglobulinemia): 1. Purpura (palpable), 2. Arthralgia, 3. Weakness. Highly associated with Hepatitis C infection.
