Pleural & Ascitic Fluid: Lightundefineds Criteria & SAAG

Exudate vs Transudate and Portal Hypertension evaluation rules...

Critical Lab Values · CSF & Fluids

Learning note: Educational revision content; verify medication doses, protocols and reference ranges against current institutional guidelines before any patient-care use.

Light's Criteria (Pleural Fluid): Fluid is an Exudate if ANY ONE of the following is true:
1. Pleural fluid protein / Serum protein ratio > 0.5
2. Pleural fluid LDH / Serum LDH ratio > 0.6
3. Pleural fluid LDH > 2/3 the upper limit of normal serum LDH.
Transudates: Heart Failure, Cirrhosis, Nephrotic Syndrome.
Exudates: Pneumonia (parapneumonic), Malignancy, Tuberculosis, Pulmonary Embolism.

Serum-Ascites Albumin Gradient (SAAG):
SAAG = Serum Albumin - Ascitic Fluid Albumin.
• High SAAG (≥ 1.1 g/dL) = Portal Hypertension present: Cirrhosis, Budd-Chiari syndrome, Congestive Heart Failure.
• Low SAAG (< 1.1 g/dL) = Normal Portal Pressure: Peritoneal Carcinomatosis, Peritoneal Tuberculosis, Pancreatitis, Nephrotic syndrome.
