Classic Chest X-Ray (CXR) Signs

Golden S, Deep Sulcus, and signs of Pulmonary Embolism on plain film...

Triads & Signs · Radiological Signs

  • Deep Sulcus Sign: Abnormally deepened and hyperlucent costophrenic angle on a supine CXR. Signifies: Pneumothorax (air collects anteriorly and basally in supine position).
  • Golden S Curve of San Felix: Reverse 'S' shape of the right horizontal fissure. Signifies: Right upper lobe collapse caused by a central obstructing mass (like bronchogenic carcinoma).
  • Hampton's Hump: Wedge-shaped peripheral opacity abutting the pleura. Signifies: Pulmonary infarction due to Pulmonary Embolism (PE).
  • Westermark Sign: Focal area of oligemia (reduced vascular markings) distal to a massive PE.
  • Water Bottle Heart: Symmetrically enlarged, globular heart shadow. Signifies: Massive Pericardial Effusion.
