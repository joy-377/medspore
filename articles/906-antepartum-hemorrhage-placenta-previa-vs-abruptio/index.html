Antepartum Hemorrhage: Placenta Previa vs Abruptio

Painless vs painful vaginal bleeding, Couvelaire uterus, and ultrasound rules...

Final Year (Part 2) · OBG

Placenta Previa:
• Definition: Placenta implanted partially or completely over the internal cervical os.
• Bleeding Character: Painless, Causeless, Recurrent, bright red vaginal bleeding.
• Uterine Tone: Soft, relaxed, non-tender uterus; fetal parts easily palpable; fetal heart rate (FHR) usually normal.
• ⚠️ Absolute Rule: Digital vaginal examination (per vaginum) is STRICTLY CONTRAINDICATED until placenta previa is ruled out by ultrasound (can trigger fatal hemorrhage).
• Investigation of Choice: Transvaginal Sonography (TVS) — completely safe and accurate.

Abruptio Placentae (Placental Abruption):
• Definition: Premature separation of a normally situated placenta before delivery.
• Risk Factors: Maternal hypertension/preeclampsia (most common), trauma, sudden uterine decompression, cocaine use.
• Bleeding Character: Painful, Dark red vaginal bleeding (revealed or concealed).
• Uterine Tone: Tense, Tender, Rigid ('Woody hard') uterus; difficult to palpate fetal parts; fetal distress or fetal demise common.
• Complications: Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC) due to thromboplastin release into maternal circulation; Couvelaire Uterus (widespread extravasation of blood into the myometrium and beneath the peritoneal coat).
