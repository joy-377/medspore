01

Clinical syndrome and red flags

  • Septic arthritis in a child is an urgent joint infection that may present with fever, severe pain and refusal to bear weight.
  • Transient synovitis may follow a viral illness and often has less systemic illness, but presentations overlap.
02

Differentiate and manage

  • Inflammatory markers, ultrasound and joint aspiration decisions require clinical context and specialist assessment.
  • Delay in septic arthritis diagnosis risks joint destruction and systemic infection.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not rule out septic arthritis merely because a child has a recent viral history.
