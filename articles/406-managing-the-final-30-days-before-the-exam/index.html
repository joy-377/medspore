Managing the Final 30 Days Before the Exam

Use the final revision period for consolidation, error correction and sustainable timed practice.

Quick Revision

  • Prioritize recurring mistakes and previously studied high-value concepts.
  • Use short retrieval sessions, carefully selected timed tests and deliberate recovery.
  • Adjust the plan to the official upcoming examination schedule and personal performance.
01

Consolidate efficiently

  • Review your existing core resources and high-risk areas; a large last-minute source switch can create confusion.
  • Use targeted practice to distinguish “forgotten knowledge” from “misread stem” errors.
02

Protect performance

  • Schedule breaks and sleep to preserve attention and retrieval.
  • Measure progress with unfamiliar practice questions, not only notes that feel easy to recognize.
03

Exam trap

  • Do not invent a universal countdown plan that ignores each student’s starting point.
  • A last-minute flood of new material can displace well-consolidated knowledge.
