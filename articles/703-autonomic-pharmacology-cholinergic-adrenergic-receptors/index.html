G-Protein Receptor Couplings (The 'QISS and QIQ' Rule)

G-Protein Receptor Couplings (The 'QISS and QIQ' Rule):
• Gq Coupled (IP3 / DAG pathway → activates PKC & increases intracellular Ca2+): Alpha-1, M1, M3, H1, V1.
• Gi Coupled (Inhibits Adenylyl Cyclase → decreases cAMP): Alpha-2, M2 (cardiac), D2.
• Gs Coupled (Stimulates Adenylyl Cyclase → increases cAMP): Beta-1 (cardiac), Beta-2 (smooth muscle relaxation), Beta-3, D1, H2, V2.

02

High-Yield Clinical Therapeutics

High-Yield Clinical Therapeutics:
• Glaucoma: Latanoprost (PGF2alpha analogue - increases uveoscleral outflow, first line); Timolol (decreases aqueous humor production); Pilocarpine (M3 agonist, contracts ciliary muscle & opens trabecular meshwork).
• Myasthenia Gravis: Pyridostigmine (long-acting AChE inhibitor) for maintenance. Edrophonium historically used for Tensilon test.
• Beta-Blocker Selectivity: Cardioselective (Beta-1 specific): Metoprolol, Atenolol, Bisoprolol, Esmolol (ultra-short acting, half-life 9 min). Non-selective with alpha-1 blocking activity: Carvedilol, Labetalol.
