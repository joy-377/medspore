Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 November 2026. This policy describes the functionality of the MedSpore website and installable web app.

Information you provide

You may enter a display name, optional profile photo, study settings, personal question notes and answers. An optional Firebase account may collect an email address, authentication identifier and information required to complete Google or email sign-in.

Local storage

The app stores practice attempts, bookmarks, settings, badges, history and other learning progress in browser local storage so guest mode works without signing in. Profile pictures chosen in this version remain local to your browser.

Optional cloud synchronization

If you sign in and Firestore access is correctly configured, the app can synchronize a snapshot of your learning data under your authenticated user account. User-specific Firebase security rules are designed to prevent other normal users from reading or writing your private documents. Administrators and service providers may have operational access as described by their policies.

Notifications

In-app notifications can appear without browser push permission. Device push requires an explicit browser permission request. If you register for push, a device token may be stored in Firestore for your account; a trusted server-side sender is necessary to deliver scheduled push messages. Browser settings can revoke permission.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

Firebase/Google services may process technical and authentication information. This release contains an AdSense publisher verification tag and an ads.txt entry inherited from the older owner-provided project. It does not intentionally display ad units. If advertisements or additional analytics are activated later, this policy and any required consent mechanisms must be reviewed and updated.

Storage, retention and deletion

Use Settings → Reset All Local Data to remove local state from this device. Local reset does not remove optional cloud account data; contact us to request cloud data deletion. App technical caches can be removed through browser site settings.

Security and children

Use a strong password and keep sign-in credentials private. The app is intended for students and adults; individuals under the age of majority should follow appropriate guardian and school policies.
