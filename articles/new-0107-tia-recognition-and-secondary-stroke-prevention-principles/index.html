Clinical syndrome and red flags

  • TIA is transient focal neurological dysfunction from ischemia without evidence of acute infarction in the modern tissue-based concept.
  • Even resolved symptoms may warn of early stroke risk, requiring urgent evaluation.
02

Differentiate and manage

  • Neurovascular imaging, cardiac rhythm evaluation and vascular risk management inform secondary prevention.
  • Differentials include migraine aura, seizure and metabolic mimics.
03

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not discharge a resolved unilateral weakness episode as harmless solely because the examination is now normal.
