High-Yield Fracture Eponyms & Nerve Pairings

Colles, Smith, Monteggia, Galeazzi fractures and nerve vulnerability...

Final Year (Part 2) · Orthopedics

Forearm Fracture-Dislocations (The 'MUGR' Rule):
• Monteggia Fracture: Fracture of the proximal 1/3rd of the Ulna with anterior dislocation of the Radial head. Nerve at risk: Posterior Interosseous Nerve (PIN).
• Galeazzi Fracture: Fracture of the junction of middle and distal 1/3rd of the Radius with dislocation of the Distal Radioulnar Joint (DRUJ). Nerve at risk: Anterior Interosseous Nerve (AIN) or Ulnar nerve.

Distal Radius Fractures:
• Colles Fracture: Extra-articular fracture of distal radius within 2 cm of articular surface with Dorsal displacement, dorsal tilt, and radial shift. Classical Dinner fork deformity. Caused by fall on outstretched hand (FOOSH).
• Smith Fracture (Reverse Colles): Fracture of distal radius with Volar (palmar) displacement and volar tilt. Garden spade deformity. Caused by fall on flexed wrist.

Upper Limb Fracture & Nerve Association Master List:
• Surgical Neck of Humerus: Axillary Nerve (loss of deltoid abduction & lateral shoulder patch sensation).
• Mid-Shaft Humerus: Radial Nerve in spiral groove (Wrist Drop).
• Supracondylar Fracture of Humerus: Median Nerve (AIN branch) most commonly; risk of Brachial Artery injury leading to Volkmann's Ischemic Contracture (VIC).
• Medial Epicondyle of Humerus: Ulnar Nerve.
