Quick Revision

  • Absence of fever, rash or a classic triad does not exclude every serious disease.
  • Use positive discriminating findings, risk factors and the stem's actual question together.

Recognize the question type

  • A negative finding helps only if it meaningfully changes the differential in the relevant clinical context.
  • Ask whether the test or examination sign is sensitive enough to rule out a condition.

Use this in timed practice

Exam trap — avoid this mistake

  • Do not overinterpret a single absent sign as proof that a diagnosis is impossible.
